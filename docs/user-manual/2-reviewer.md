# 2. The reviewer

[Back to the manual](README.md)

> **In short:** The reviewer starts with the entities most at risk, checks each figure against its
> evidence, and either approves a submission or sends back only the figures they doubt. Nobody can
> edit a figure, not even the reviewer.

**Signs in as:** DSAC reviewer (L. Dlamini).

**The journey:** see what needs attention today, open the queue ranked by risk, find out why an
entity is ranked where it is, send one of Iziko's Q1 figures back, approve Boxing South Africa, and
look at the risk picture across all 28 entities.

---

## Step 1. Today

After signing in you land on **Today**. It answers one question: what to look at first.

![Today: four counts, the three highest-risk entities, quick actions and tasks](img/j3-01-today.png)

- **Four counts** across the top: submissions **awaiting your decision**, figures **returned** to
  an entity, entities that have **filed nothing**, and submissions **approved**.
- **Start with these three**: the three entities at the top of the risk ranking, each with the
  single **largest factor** behind its score, written in words.
- On the right, **quick actions** and **My tasks**.

Click **The whole queue, ranked**.

## Step 2. The review queue, ranked by risk

The queue lists every entity for Q1, **ranked by risk rather than by the date it arrived**, so the
ones that most need attention are at the top.

![The review queue with the risk score (1) and the entity (2) marked](img/j3-02-queue.png)

1. The **risk score** and its band. The number and the word are always shown, not only the colour.
2. The **entity**, its status (Submitted, Returned, Approved or not submitted), its largest factor,
   and how many figures and evidence documents it filed and how late.

## Step 3. Ask why an entity is ranked where it is

Click Robben Island's score. A panel lists the **five signals** behind it, each with its weight,
what it adds to the total, and a sentence explaining it.

![Robben Island's score explained: five signals, each with its weight and contribution](img/j3-03-why.png)

The five signals are: how late it files, how much of its reporting has no evidence, whether
spending is running ahead of delivery, its audit findings, and whether it changed its targets
mid-year. The score is arithmetic, not a guess: anyone can add it up from this panel. Robben Island
scores 45 because it filed 70 days after a legal deadline and its audit is outstanding.

Close the panel.

## Step 4. Open Iziko's Q1 submission

Find Iziko Museums in the queue and click **open** (1).

![Iziko in the queue with open marked](img/j3-04-iziko-in-queue.png)

The submission opens with who filed it, when, how late, and how many figures have evidence. Each
figure sits beside where it came from (a spreadsheet cell, or **Entered by hand**), the entity's
own explanation, and its evidence documents. Click a document to read it without leaving the page.

![Iziko's Q1 submission: each figure with its targets, variance, explanation and evidence](img/j3-05-submission.png)

At the bottom, a bar says what will happen. With nothing marked, its one button is **Approve all 20
figures**.

![Nothing is marked, with Approve all 20 figures](img/j3-08a-approve-state.png)

## Step 5. Dispute one figure

HER-1.1, exhibitions mounted, says 2. The attached register lists three exhibitions.

1. Click **Dispute this figure** under HER-1.1.
2. Write the reason. Iziko will see it against this figure only, so say exactly what is wrong.
3. Click **Mark disputed** (1).

![The dispute box on HER-1.1 with the reason written and Mark disputed marked](img/j3-06-dispute.png)

The figure now carries a **DISPUTED** label with the reason underneath. **Nothing has gone to Iziko
yet.** **Edit the reason** and **Undo this dispute** are there if you change your mind.

![HER-1.1 marked as disputed](img/j3-07-disputed.png)

## Step 6. Return the disputed figure

The bar at the bottom has changed. It says one figure is marked, and its button is now **Return 1
figure to the entity** (1).

![One figure is marked, with Return 1 figure to the entity marked](img/j3-08-actions.png)

Click it. Vuka lists the disputed figure with your reason. Add an overall note if it helps, and
click **Return 1 figure**.

![The Return this submission dialog](img/j3-09-return-modal.png)

You are back in the queue, and a line at the top confirms what was recorded. Only HER-1.1 goes back
to Iziko; the other 19 figures stay exactly as filed, with their original author.

![The queue: returned to the entity with one figure to correct](img/j3-09b-returned.png)

## Step 7. Approve Boxing South Africa

Open **Boxing South Africa** from the queue. Satisfied with it, click **Approve all 18 figures**. The
dialog warns how many figures have no evidence: approving does not change that, and they stay on
the record as unverifiable. Click **Approve**.

![The Approve this submission dialog for Boxing South Africa](img/j3-10-approve-modal.png)

Back in the queue, the line at the top confirms it, and Boxing South Africa reads **Approved**.
Approval is recorded with the reviewer's name and the time, one submission at a time.

![The queue: approved, Boxing South Africa is off the queue](img/j3-11-approved.png)

## Step 8. The risk picture across all 28

Click **Risk & Alerts** in the menu. This is the early warning view: how many entities are in each
band, and every entity with its band, its largest factor, its score, and whether it has reported.

![Risk & Alerts: counts by band, the distribution, and every entity's largest factor](img/j3-12-risk.png)

The bands are fixed and published: 70 or above is critical, 50 to 69 high, 25 to 49 medium, below 25
low. The reviewer's journey ends here.
